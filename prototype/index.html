Ambient AI scribe for clinics

The doctor talks to the patient. The note writes itself.

ClinicScribe listens to the consultation, transcribes it with a medical speech model, and drafts the clinical note in the doctor's format. Every line of the note links back to what was actually said, so the doctor stays in control and nothing is invented.

How it works

Four steps, no change to how the doctor consults

Step 1

Consent and record

One tap on the phone. A scripted consent line is shown, the patient's agreement is logged with a timestamp. Pause and resume any time.

Step 2

Medical transcription

A speech model trained on clinical audio separates Doctor and Patient and spells drug names, doses and investigations correctly. The clinic's own term list sharpens it further.

Step 3

Draft note with evidence

A language model writes the note in the chosen template. Each statement carries the transcript lines it came from. Anything without a source is flagged, and topics not discussed are listed instead of guessed.

Step 4

Review, sign, export

The doctor edits any section, regenerates one if needed, signs, and copies the note or a PDF into the EMR. Audio is deleted automatically after signing.

What doctors get

Built around the things clinicians actually ask for

These are the features in the one-month prototype. Each one is shown working in the demo app with a fictional consultation.

Ambient recording

Record in the room or on a video call from the phone browser. Pause for sensitive moments. Chunked upload so a dropped connection loses nothing.

Why: zero setup, works on the phone the doctor already carries.

Dictation mode

For procedures and quick follow-ups, the doctor narrates the note. Same templates, same review flow, no speaker separation needed.

Why: many clinicians prefer dictating; both habits are supported.

Speaker-labelled medical transcript

Doctor and Patient turns with timestamps. Medical vocabulary such as Metformin 1000 mg BD or HbA1c 7.8% comes through correctly.

Why: the note is only as good as the transcript.

Note templates

SOAP, Follow-up and General consult at launch. Section names and order are configurable per doctor.

Why: doctors reject notes that are not in their own format.

Evidence linking

Click any line of the note and the transcript jumps to the sentences it came from. Lines with no source are highlighted for the doctor to remove or confirm.

Why: this is what makes doctors trust the AI. It is the core of the product.

No invention

A second check verifies every statement against the transcript. Topics that were not discussed are listed as "not addressed" rather than filled in.

Why: a made-up allergy or dose is a safety issue, not a typo.

Edit, regenerate, sign

Rewrite any section by hand or ask for a fresh draft of just that section. Signing locks the note and starts the audio deletion timer.

Why: the note is always the doctor's. The AI only drafts.

Export anywhere

Copy the whole note or one section with a click, or download a formatted PDF. Works with any EMR that has a text field.

Why: EMR integration is slow and costly; copy-paste gets doctors using it on day one.

Audio auto-delete

Raw audio is deleted 24 hours after the note is signed. The transcript and note are kept. The doctor can delete a whole visit at any time.

Why: minimal data kept, which is what patients and privacy law expect.

Clinic vocabulary

A list of drugs, procedures and local terms per doctor that is sent to the speech model so it recognises them first time.

Why: cheap way to lift accuracy for a specialty without training a model.

Consent logged per visit

A scripted disclosure, a checkbox, and a timestamp stored with the visit. Patients can decline and the doctor charts manually.

Why: recording a patient without documented consent is the biggest legal risk in this category.

Visit history

All past visits with status, template and timing, searchable by patient reference. Audio status is always visible.

Why: doctors look back at the last note before the next visit.
Scope

What the one-month prototype includes, and what comes after approval

In the prototype (4 weeks)

  • Web app that works on the phone browser, installable to the home screen
  • Consent step, recording with pause, chunked upload
  • Medical speech-to-text with speaker labels and clinic vocabulary
  • Dictation mode
  • SOAP, Follow-up and General consult templates
  • Evidence-linked note, unsupported-line flags, not-addressed list
  • Section editor, regenerate section, sign
  • Copy and PDF export, visit history, audio auto-delete
  • Deployed on the clinic's own accounts, source code handed over

After approval (phase 2 onward)

  • Native iOS and Android apps
  • Learning each doctor's writing style from their edits
  • Hindi and regional-language consultations
  • Patient-friendly visit summary and follow-up reminders
  • ICD-10 coding suggestions
  • EMR / HMIS integration
  • Clinic admin, multiple doctors, usage reporting
  • Security review and compliance documentation (DPDP / HIPAA)
About this demo. Everything you see in the demo app uses fictional patients and pre-written sample data so the flow can be reviewed before any speech or language model is connected. After approval the same screens are wired to live transcription and note generation.
