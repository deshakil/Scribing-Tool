How it works

Three steps. Nothing changes about how you consult.

1

Record or upload

Ask the patient, tap Record on your phone. Pause any time. Or upload an existing recording, or dictate the note yourself after the visit.

2

Review the draft

Within a minute you get a note in your template. Tap any line to see the exact words it came from. Lines with no source are flagged.

3

Sign and copy

Edit what you want, sign, and copy the note into your EMR. The audio is deleted automatically.

Features

Everything a busy clinic needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Record, dictate or upload

Record the consultation live, dictate afterwards, or upload a recording you already have. All three lead to the same note.

Medical transcript

Doctor and patient turns separated, drug names and doses spelled correctly, your own clinic vocabulary recognised.

Your templates

SOAP, Follow-up and General consult out of the box. Sections and order set per doctor.

Every line has a source

Tap a line in the note and the transcript jumps to the sentences behind it. Nothing is written that was not said.

Edit, regenerate, sign

Rewrite any section or ask for a fresh draft of just that section. Signing locks the note.

Copy or PDF

One click to copy the whole note or a section into any EMR. Formatted PDF for the file.

Built for trust

Patients and doctors stay in control

Consent, every visit

A short script to read to the patient and a logged consent before recording starts. Patients can decline.

No invented findings

Every statement is checked against the transcript. What was not discussed is listed as not addressed, not filled in.

Audio deleted

The recording is deleted 24 hours after you sign. Only the transcript and note are kept, and you can delete a visit at any time.

Log in with the demo account, open a visit, and tap through the note. Everything you see uses fictional patients.
